WHAT’S NEXT FOR US?

Upcoming Competitions (2026 & 2027) :

We aim to participate in the following prestigious aerospace and UAV competitions, representing our institution at multiple levels:

  • NACDEC 2026 – Organized by the Aeronautical Society of India
  • ISRO IROC (International Rover Challenge – ISRO Track)
  • NITK Drone Racing Competition 2026
  • SAE International Aero Design West, 2027
  • SAE Aerothon 2027

Research & Development Initiatives: (Ongoing and Upcoming)

  • Indigenous Flight Controller Development – End-to-end hardware and firmware design
  • Electronic Speed Controller (ESC) Development – High-efficiency propulsion electronics
  • GPS-Denied Navigation Systems – Vision- and sensor-based autonomous navigation
  • Surveillance Aircraft Development – Re-configurable Multi-Mission Fixed-Wing UAV with Convertible Propulsion and Integrated Modular Payload Bay.
  • Confidential Defense Project – In collaboration with DRDO (details restricted)
  • Morphing Wingtip Mechanisms – Adaptive aerodynamics for performance optimization
  • Directional Stability Enhancement Using Deployable Micro-Fins – Active stability control for UAV platforms

Strategic Project Status

  • 100% of the Research, Development, and Technical Fabrication for our current flight systems is subsidized by R&D department at BMS College of Engineering.
  • We have a flight-proven product ready for the world stage.

How Our Partners Enable This

  • Financial Sponsorship – Support for travel, accommodation, logistics
  • Academic & Research Guidance
